Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How does automatic CGST, SGST, and IGST tax calculation work? ▼

The system compares your registered Company State code (e.g. Kerala - 32) with your customer's state code. If both are in the same state (Intra-State), tax is automatically split equally into CGST (9%) + SGST (9%). If the states differ (Inter-State), the engine applies IGST (18%).

Can I upload the GSTR-1 JSON directly to gst.gov.in? ▼

Yes! Go to Reports → GSTR-1 tab → Select Tax Month → Click "Download GST Portal JSON". Then log into gst.gov.in → Returns Dashboard → GSTR-1 → Prepare Offline → Upload the JSON file.

What happens when I issue a Credit Note for returned goods? ▼

Issuing a Credit Note automatically adjusts the customer's outstanding balance and restores the returned item quantity directly back into your active warehouse inventory.

Can I import my data from Tally or Excel spreadsheets? ▼

Yes! Navigate to Previous Data Import in the portal. Download our standard CSV template for Customers or Items, paste your data, and click Upload to import thousands of records in seconds.
