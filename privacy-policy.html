Privacy Policy

Updated | March 2026 | Scope: Official Corporate Privacy Statement

1. Commitment to User Privacy

At Eebii Technologies ("Eebii", "we", "our"), protecting your confidential business data and privacy is our paramount priority. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling principles across the Eebii ecosystem, including our corporate portal (eebii.com), enterprise billing suite (pro.eebii.com), free web utilities, and IT services.

2. In-Browser Client-Side Processing Guarantee

A core architectural principle of Eebii’s free business tools is local browser execution. When you use our utilities:

  • Free GST Invoice Generator (invoice.eebii.com): Your customer details, line items, bank accounts, and invoice PDFs are processed inside your browser memory using JavaScript. Zero invoice data is uploaded, transmitted, or stored on Eebii servers.
  • Barcode & Label Designer (eebii.com/barcode-generator.html): Barcode numbers, retail prices, store titles, and PNG/SVG exports are created purely client-side using JavaScript canvas and SVG rendering. No SKU or price data ever leaves your computer.
  • Cloud PDF Suite (pdfsuite.eebii.com): Documents merged, compressed, or transformed are processed via WebAssembly and in-browser buffers with zero cloud persistence.

3. Information We Collect When You Interact With Us

We collect limited information strictly necessary to provide and manage our services:

  • Contact & Inquiry Data: When you submit our contact or service request form, we collect your name, email address, optional telephone number, and message content to respond to your technical request.
  • Cloud Account Registration (Eebii Invoice Pro): If you choose to subscribe to our multi-device cloud software at pro.eebii.com, we securely process account credentials and authorized business details under encrypted database protocols.
  • Eebii Academy Enrollment: When enrolling in courses, student names, contact information, and course progress data are stored to administer class schedules and certifications.
  • Technical Telemetry: Standard non-personally identifiable log information such as browser type, operating system, referring URL, and page timestamps to analyze site speed and security.

4. How We Safeguard Your Information

We deploy modern security controls including mandatory HTTPS with 256-bit TLS encryption, strict Content Security Policies, hardened cloud servers, and restricted employee access protocols to protect all operational data against unauthorized access.

5. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Policy

To support the development and free maintenance of our knowledge articles and public utilities, Eebii displays non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdSense and authorized third-party ad networks:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

6. Cookies and Web Storage

We may use cookies and browser local storage (LocalStorage / SessionStorage) to preserve user preferences (such as your chosen barcode label presets or UI settings) across sessions. You can configure your browser to reject cookies, though certain preference-saving functions may be affected.

7. Data Rights and Privacy Inquiries

Under applicable Indian data protection standards and international privacy norms, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you have provided to us. For inquiries or data requests, please contact our Data Protection Officer at contact@eebii.com.
